The Big Takeaway

Technology-assisted agriculture for smarter crop health management.

What We Learned

One leaf photo can give a fast first check of crop health. Image classification and an advice database work together, and transfer learning keeps training and deployment manageable.

What's Next

Field testing and expert validation come before real-world use. Soil, weather and voice support in local languages are natural next steps.
