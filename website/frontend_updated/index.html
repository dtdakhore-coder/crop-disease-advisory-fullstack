Why Farmers Need This

  • ✕ Late Detection — disease spreads silently before visible damage is noticed.
  • ✕ Confusing Symptoms — fungal and bacterial leaf spots look identical.
  • ✕ Expert Scarcity — field agronomists cannot visit every farm on time.
  • ✓ CropCare Solution: Instant 2-second AI screening right in the field.

End-to-End Precision Architecture

A cohesive 3-stage intelligence engine designed for rapid farm field triage.

1. Vision & Gatekeeper

Image normalization, color space balancing, and ImageNet zero-shot gatekeeper to verify actual leaf foliage.

2. Deep Neural Classifier

Transfer learning via MobileNetV2 with global average pooling to diagnose disease class and probability.

3. Agronomic Advisory

Automated expert knowledge mapping that generates specific fertilizer NPK formulations, nutrient corrections, and care tips.

Core Agricultural Impact

Engineered to support farmers, agricultural students, and field extension officers.

⚡ Instant Diagnosis

Get results in under 2 seconds directly from your phone camera.

🛡️ Anti-False Positive

Built-in gatekeeper rejects non-crop photos like pets and objects.

🧪 NPK Recommendations

Custom fertilizer advice tailored to the diagnosed condition.

🌐 Multilingual Access

Accessible in English, Hindi, and Marathi for local usability.

Agro-Advisory Notice: This system is designed as a primary decision-support and screening aid. For severe field epidemics, always corroborate recommendations with local agricultural extension officers or certified agronomists.
