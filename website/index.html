ML-Based Crop Disease Detection & Fertilizer Advisory System

An ML-Based Smart Agriculture Decision-Support System. Photograph a leaf, get a quick first opinion on the disease, and receive matched fertilizer and crop-care advice.

Try the Live Demo How It Works

Why this problem matters

  • ✗ Spotted too late — damage spreads before symptoms are obvious
  • ✗ Diseases look alike — similar marks on the leaf
  • ✗ No expert nearby — quick advice is hard to get in the field
  • ✗ Money lost — wrong spray, wasted fertilizer, smaller harvest

Our Idea in One Picture

Three parts working as a team: See, Decide, Advise.

1. See — Computer Vision

Picks out useful patterns from the leaf photo using image preprocessing and feature extraction.

2. Decide — Deep Learning

Transfer learning with MobileNetV2 / EfficientNet names the most likely disease and confidence.

3. Advise — Knowledge Base

A rule-based database matches crop + disease to fertilizer, nutrient and care tips — easy to update by experts.

Benefits & Where It Fits

What farmers and students get from it.

Quick Screening

A fast first-level identification of common crop diseases from a single photo.

Easy to Use

Only a photograph is needed — no lab equipment or expert required at first check.

Matched Advice

Fertilizer, nutrient and care tips are tied to the actual detected crop and disease.

Built to Grow

Can expand to more crops, diseases, and Marathi / Hindi language support later.

Honest limitation: Results depend on photo quality and training data. Similar diseases can look alike, advice is general guidance not a prescription, and real use needs field testing and expert review. This system supports a farmer's decision — it does not replace an agronomist.
